Spikewise

Which NSE stocks moved because of real news, and which just moved with the market?

How it works. Every trading day Spikewise removes the part of each stock's move that NIFTY 50 explains (rolling 20-day beta). A day is flagged as a spike only when the remaining move is unusually large (z-score above 1.5) and volume is above 1.25× its 20-day average. Each spike is matched to the nearest official NSE/BSE corporate announcement.
Coverage. About 450 NSE stocks, refreshed after market close.
