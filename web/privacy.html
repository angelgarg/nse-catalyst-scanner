Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026 · Applies to the Spikewise Android app and this website.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you, by default. Your watchlist, alert settings and preferences are stored only on your device. Spikewise has no accounts and no sign-in.

Feedback you choose to send

If you use Send feedback, we receive the message you type, an optional rating, the app version, the screen you were on, and an email address only if you enter one. Feedback is stored as an issue in our GitHub project so it can be fixed; do not include personal or financial details in it. The email address, if given, is used only to reply to you and is not published.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. Google tells the app whether your subscription is active.

Network requests

The app downloads market data from our servers (GitHub Pages and Render). These providers may log IP addresses for security and operations, as any web server does. We do not use advertising IDs, analytics SDKs or tracking.

Children

Spikewise is not directed at children under 18.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device. To delete feedback you sent, email us.
